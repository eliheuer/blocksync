# Independent visual review

Use a reviewer who did not draft the glyphs. Give the reviewer the before and after proofs at matching axes, size and baseline, the names and masters, and CONTEXT.md. Keep the writer’s rationale separate until the reviewer has recorded a verdict.

Examine silhouettes, counters, joins, overshoot, spacing and mark placement at text and display sizes. Read the two poster titles in the two-axis proof. Inspect all affected composites beside their bases and decomposed sequences. Compare extremes and an intermediate location. Record pass, fault or designer decision for each assigned glyph and master, with the visible location and proof page. A verdict covers only the named batch. Only the designer can change a grade to green.
