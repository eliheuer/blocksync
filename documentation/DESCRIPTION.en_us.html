Blocksync is a block-like variable display typeface by Eli Heuer. The family uses a modular construction with hard-edged forms and supports width and weight variation for compact, normal, and expanded display settings.

The Google Fonts release source is maintained as UFO masters plus a designspace, with the historical Glyphs sources archived for reference. Contributions and source files are available at github.com/eliheuer/blocksync.
